Measurements use exactly one complete denoising-step invocation, no warmup. Headline cycles are the runner rdcycle delta, excluding output/trace serialization. 60 MHz is the configured target frequency. Plain/trace observed difference combines instrumentation cost and run variation.

Gemmini occupancy is sum of synchronous Gemmini dispatch wall intervals / trace invoke cycles. Those intervals include Rocket issue and wait; they are not accelerator hardware busy counters. Useful MAC counts use the product of dimensions parsed from each executed Gemmini function name, per perfX. Gemmini MAC utilization divides useful MACs by 256 * Gemmini dispatch cycles (16x16 array); overall divides by 256 * full invoke cycles. DMA/execute/store phases are not measured. Dispatch counts classify emitted Gemmini instructions versus Rocket scalar assembly. Weighted compile coverage443/3 does not predict all runtime dispatch counts.
